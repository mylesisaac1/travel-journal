About This App

Welcome to My Travel Journal, a simple web application that helps you document and remember your travel adventures.

Features

  • Add travel entries with location, date, description, and photos
  • View all your entries in a beautiful timeline
  • Search and filter your entries
  • Your data is saved locally in your browser

Travel Tips

Packing Essentials

Always pack light but don't forget important documents, medications, and chargers.

Cultural Awareness

Research local customs and etiquette before visiting a new place.

Safety First

Keep digital copies of important documents and share your itinerary with someone.
